Lineup · what it does

Features

Live sports & teams

Follow the NFL, NBA, NHL, MLB, college football and basketball, soccer, tennis, UFC, Formula 1 and NASCAR. Check schedules, live scores and team pages, then jump straight to the channel showing a game, or to your provider’s catch-up when it has one. On iPhone, a game that hasn’t started opens its own page, with each side’s recent form and every channel showing it across your playlists. If a channel won’t start, Lineup moves on to the next one and remembers which channels let you down. NFL teams you follow also get Game Replays. Hide the sports you don’t watch, and choose which Top 25 shows next to college teams.

Live TV guide

Search channels and listings, keep your favorites in your own order, jump back to what you watched recently, and start live TV or a catch-up recording right from the guide. The guide opens on your channel categories, with multi-toggle category management synced between iPhone and Apple TV. On iPhone, you can also combine several IPTV providers with a section for each, and watch two channels side by side.

Media servers & progress

Connect Jellyfin, Emby, Silo or an AIOStreams server, and add your IPTV provider’s movies and shows. Browse everything together, or one server at a time, laid out the way its own app does it. Jellyfin signs in with Quick Connect, and on iPhone you can switch between household profiles in Settings. A title on two servers shows up once, and Continue Watching stays in step through your server, MDBList, or both. Seerr handles discovery and requests, down to single seasons.

Streams & badges

The stream list shows each stream the way your server’s formatter writes it. Add badge packs made for AIOStreams, Fusion or Nuvio to put quality and audio badges on every stream, above or below the text. Streams start loading as soon as you open a title, so they’re ready when you tap Play.

Library, collections & artwork

Choose where titles get their details: your server, TMDB or TVDB, with your server’s own posters if you prefer them. Arrange your Library rows, mix several catalogs into the hero, and watch the streaming-service posters come to life. On iPhone, build your own collections, filter them to movies or shows, and find them in Search. Title logos and posters come from Fanart.tv, TMDB and BetterPosters, and ratings from IMDb, TMDB, Rotten Tomatoes, Metacritic and Letterboxd show up where they’re available.

Playback

On iPhone, play with AetherEngine or MPVKit behind Liquid Glass controls. Apple TV also offers VLC. Ambient Mode spreads the picture’s colors into the black bars, like YouTube does, on both. Depending on the device and stream, you can follow live stats with Auto Sync, watch two games in multiview, search for subtitles, or skip an episode’s intro. On iPhone, picture-in-picture keeps the game going while you use another app, and the screen lock ignores stray touches and keeps the picture from turning.

Built for each screen

Lineup has touch-first iPhone controls, a side tab layout that fills the screen on iPhone Duo, with the picture on the top half when it’s folded like a laptop, and a remote-friendly Apple TV interface. To set up an Apple TV, scan the code it shows with your iPhone: your providers, servers, sign-ins and watch history come straight across, with nothing to type on the remote.

NullSign · what it does

Features

Sign on your device

Use your own .p12 certificate and matching .mobileprovision profile to sign an IPA right on your iPhone. Your certificate never gets uploaded to a remote signing service.

Import apps easily

Bring IPAs and TIPAs in from the Files app or a URL, a few at a time if you like. Apps keep their real names, versions and icons, and if an import fails you’ll see why.

Install in one flow

Tap Install to sign an app with your saved certificate and settings. One progress bar follows it from preparing to installed, with Retry if something goes wrong. Installs go over your Wi-Fi, or through the phone itself when there’s no Wi-Fi. iOS still asks you to confirm.

Updates in one tap

NullSign checks your sources for newer versions of your apps and shows a count on the Signer tab. Tap Update and it downloads the new version, signs it with the same certificate and installs it over the old one. NullSign updates itself the same way. Turn on Keep Only the Newest Version to clear out old copies automatically.

Made for iPhone Duo

NullSign fills the whole screen on iPhone Duo, with the tab bar on the right like the rest of the phone. It uses the standard iOS tab bar everywhere, so tapping the tab you’re on takes you back to its first screen.

Manage signing identities

Save several certificate and profile pairs, name them, check them, pick the one you need, or remove an old one. Passwordless .p12 files work, and you’ll get a clear message for a wrong password or a profile that doesn’t match.

Browse app sources

Add AltStore-compatible repositories to browse their apps and releases from inside NullSign. Versions show their build number, like 0.19.0 (960), so you can tell builds apart.

Optional tweaks

Import .deb and .dylib tweaks for supported signing workflows. ElleKit is on by default for new or reset signing settings, and you can change it in signing options.

NullSign on iPhone → Apple TV

Sideload onto Apple TV

You need NullSign 2.0 on your iPhone, a tvOS IPA, and a tvOS signing certificate and profile valid for your Apple TV. Your iPhone profile alone won’t work. Browse certificates: Flarestore ↗ · Arcticsign ↗. Use your Apple TV’s UDID. Confirm tvOS support with Arcticsign before ordering.

  1. Connect both devices.

    Put your iPhone and Apple TV on the same Wi-Fi network. Keep Apple TV awake.

  2. Open pairing on Apple TV.

    Go to Settings → Remotes and Devices → Remote App and Devices. Leave that screen open.

  3. Pair in NullSign.

    On iPhone, open NullSign → Settings → Apple TV. Allow Local Network access when iOS asks. Tap your TV, enter the six-digit code it shows and tap Pair. NullSign remembers the pairing, so you only do this once.

  4. Add your TV certificate.

    Open Settings → Certificates → +. Add your .p12, its tvOS .mobileprovision and password, then tap Save. Certificates that can sign Apple TV apps show a tvOS tag.

  5. Get a tvOS app.

    For Lineup, open the Apps tab, find Lineup tvOS in Null’s Apps and tap Get. For any other app, download its Apple TV IPA, then in the Signer tab tap + → Import from Files and select it.

  6. Sign and install.

    In the Signer tab, tap Sign on the app. NullSign picks your tvOS certificate for Apple TV apps. Tap Start Signing: NullSign signs the app and installs it on your Apple TV over Wi-Fi. Keep NullSign open until the card says On Apple TV, then open the app from your Apple TV’s Home Screen.

If your TV doesn’t appear

Keep Remote App and Devices open on TV. Check both devices are on the same network, allow NullSign’s Local Network permission in iPhone Settings, then tap Scan or Refresh in Settings → Apple TV.

Restarting your Apple TV and repeating the pairing steps can also help if NullSign still cannot find it.

Apple TV pairing does not need a VPN, a computer or an imported pairing file. Those are only for installing iPhone apps with the idevice installation type.

If signing or installing stops

“Choose a certificate whose provisioning profile includes tvOS”: the selected certificate can’t sign Apple TV apps. On the Sign screen, tap the certificate and pick one with the tvOS tag, or add a tvOS certificate.

“Pair an Apple TV in Settings” or “The paired Apple TV is offline”: open Settings → Apple TV. If it says Saved • looking nearby, wake your Apple TV and check it’s on the same Wi-Fi. If it still won’t connect, tap Forget and pair again.

Steps match NullSign 2.0. Use an Apple TV build of the app; an iPhone IPA won’t install on TV. iPhone apps keep installing on your iPhone as before.
