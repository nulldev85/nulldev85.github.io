Lineup · what it does

Features

Live sports & teams

Follow the NFL, NBA, NHL, MLB, college sports, soccer, tennis, UFC, Formula 1 and NASCAR. Check schedules, live scores and team pages, then jump straight to the channel showing a game, or to your provider’s catch-up when it has one. If a channel won’t start, Lineup moves on to the next one and remembers which channels let you down. NFL teams you follow also get Game Replays.

Live TV guide

Search channels and listings from one IPTV provider or several at once, keep your favorites in your own order, and start live TV or a catch-up recording right from the guide. On iPhone, the guide opens on your channel categories, and you can watch two channels side by side.

Media servers & progress

Connect Jellyfin, Emby, Plex, Silo or an AIOStreams server, and add your IPTV provider’s movies and shows. Jellyfin signs in with Quick Connect, and on iPhone you can switch between household profiles from the Library. A title on two servers shows up once, and Continue Watching stays in step through your server, MDBList, or both. Seerr handles discovery and requests, down to single seasons.

Streams & badges

The stream list shows each stream the way your server’s formatter writes it. Add badge packs made for AIOStreams, Fusion or Nuvio to put quality and audio badges on every stream, above or below the text. Streams start loading as soon as you open a title, so they’re ready when you tap Play.

Library, collections & artwork

Choose where titles get their details: your server, TMDB or TVDB, with your server’s own posters if you prefer them. Arrange your Library rows, mix several catalogs into the hero, and watch the streaming-service posters come to life. On iPhone, build your own collections, filter them to movies or shows, and find them in Search. Title logos and posters come from Fanart.tv, TMDB and BetterPosters, and ratings from IMDb, TMDB, Rotten Tomatoes, Metacritic and Letterboxd show up where they’re available.

Playback

On iPhone, play with AetherEngine or MPVKit behind Liquid Glass controls. Apple TV also offers VLC. Ambient Mode spreads the picture’s colors into the black bars, like YouTube does, on both. Depending on the device and stream, you can follow live stats with Auto Sync, watch two games in multiview, search for subtitles, or skip an episode’s intro. On iPhone, picture-in-picture keeps the game going while you use another app.

Built for each screen

Lineup has touch-first iPhone controls, a side tab layout that fills the screen on iPhone Duo, and a remote-friendly Apple TV interface. To set up an Apple TV, scan the code it shows with your iPhone: your providers, servers, sign-ins and watch history come straight across, with nothing to type on the remote.

NullSign · what it does

Features

Sign on your device

Use your own .p12 certificate and matching .mobileprovision profile to sign an IPA right on your iPhone. Your certificate never gets uploaded to a remote signing service.

Import apps easily

Bring IPAs and TIPAs in from the Files app or a URL, a few at a time if you like. Apps keep their real names, versions and icons, and if an import fails you’ll see why.

Install in one flow

Tap Install to sign an app with your saved certificate and settings. One progress bar follows it from preparing to installed, with Retry if something goes wrong. Installs go over your Wi-Fi, or through the phone itself when there’s no Wi-Fi. iOS still asks you to confirm.

Updates in one tap

NullSign checks your sources for newer versions of your apps and shows a count on the Signer tab. Tap Update and it downloads the new version, signs it with the same certificate and installs it over the old one. NullSign updates itself the same way. Turn on Keep Only the Newest Version to clear out old copies automatically.

Made for iPhone Duo

NullSign fills the whole screen on iPhone Duo, with the tab bar on the right like the rest of the phone. It uses the standard iOS tab bar everywhere, so tapping the tab you’re on takes you back to its first screen.

Manage signing identities

Save several certificate and profile pairs, name them, check them, pick the one you need, or remove an old one. Passwordless .p12 files work, and you’ll get a clear message for a wrong password or a profile that doesn’t match.

Browse app sources

Add AltStore-compatible repositories to browse their apps and releases from inside NullSign. Versions show their build number, like 0.19.0 (960), so you can tell builds apart.

Optional tweaks

Import .deb and .dylib tweaks for supported signing workflows. ElleKit is on by default for new or reset signing settings, and you can change it in signing options.

iPhone setup

Install NullSign

Have your .p12 certificate, matching .mobileprovision for your iPhone, and certificate password ready in Files. Need these? Browse certificates: FlareStore ↗ · ArcticSign ↗.

  1. Download NullSign.

    Open this page in Safari on your iPhone. Save the IPA in Files → Downloads.

    Download NullSign IPA
  2. Open FlareStore’s signer.Open FlareStore Web Signer ↗
  3. Choose your IPA.

    Under Select IPA, choose File and select NullSign from Downloads.

  4. Add your certificate.

    Under Signing Assets, select your .mobileprovision and .p12. Enter the password if set. Leave Advanced Options alone.

  5. Sign and install.

    Tap Sign Application. On the install page, tap Install and accept the iPhone prompt. Then open NullSign from your Home Screen.

If NullSign won’t open

Untrusted Developer: Open Settings → General → VPN & Device Management. Select the signing developer and follow the Trust or Allow & Restart prompts.

Developer Mode Required: Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode. Turn it on, restart, then confirm. Do this only if iPhone asks.

Integrity or verification error: Check your internet connection and ask your certificate provider to confirm the certificate is valid and the profile covers this iPhone.

NullSign on iPhone → Apple TV

Sideload onto Apple TV

You need NullSign on your iPhone, a tvOS IPA, and a tvOS signing certificate and profile valid for your Apple TV. Your iPhone profile alone won’t work.

  1. Connect both devices.

    Put your iPhone and Apple TV on the same network. Keep Apple TV awake.

  2. Open pairing on Apple TV.

    Go to Settings → Remotes and Devices → Remote App and Devices. Leave that screen open.

  3. Pair in NullSign.

    On iPhone, open NullSign → Settings → Installation Method → Apple TV / Tunnel. Select your TV, enter its six-digit code and tap Pair.

  4. Add your TV certificate.

    In NullSign, open Settings → Certificates → +. Add your .p12, its tvOS .mobileprovision and password, then tap Save.

  5. Import a tvOS app.

    Download the Apple TV IPA for the app you want. In NullSign’s Signer tab, tap Import → Import from Files and select it.

  6. Install it.

    Tap the imported app’s Install action. Keep NullSign open until it finishes, then open the app on Apple TV.

If your TV doesn’t appear

Keep Remote App and Devices open on TV. Check both devices are on the same network, allow NullSign’s Local Network permission in iPhone Settings, then tap Scan or Refresh in NullSign.

Apple TV pairing does not need a VPN or an imported pairing file. Those controls are for the separate iPhone tunnel route.

Steps match NullSign 1.5.2. Use an Apple TV build of the app; an iPhone IPA won’t install on TV.
