Lineup

Live sports · iPhone & Apple TV

Lineup puts live sports, live TV and your own media library in one app on iPhone and Apple TV. Connect your IPTV provider and media servers, follow the teams you care about, and watch everything from one place.

Live sports & teams

Follow the NFL, NBA, NHL, MLB, college sports, soccer, tennis, UFC, Formula 1 and NASCAR. Check schedules, live scores and team pages, then jump straight to the channel showing a game, or to your provider’s catch-up when it has one. NFL teams you follow also get Game Replays.

Live TV guide

Browse and search channels and listings from one IPTV provider or several at once. Keep your favorite channels in the order you like, and start live TV or a catch-up recording right from the guide.

Media servers & progress

Connect Jellyfin, Emby, Plex or Silo, and add your IPTV provider’s movies and shows. Trending and streaming-service rows show what’s popular, and Seerr handles discovery and requests. Track progress on the device, with MDBList, or both, and pick up where you left off from Continue Watching.

Metadata & artwork

Choose where titles get their details: your server, TMDB or TVDB. Arrange your Library rows and collections, and get title logos and posters from Fanart.tv, TMDB and BetterPosters. Ratings from IMDb, TMDB, Rotten Tomatoes, Metacritic and Letterboxd show up where they’re available.

Playback tools

Pick a player for each part of the app: AetherEngine, VLC or MPVKit, plus Apple’s own player on iPhone. Depending on the device and stream, you also get live stats with Auto Sync, multiview, online subtitle search and Skip Intro for library episodes, and picture-in-picture on iPhone.

Built for each screen

Lineup has touch-first iPhone controls and a remote-friendly Apple TV interface, with settings and playback choices adapted to each platform. To set up an Apple TV, scan the code it shows with your iPhone: your providers, servers, sign-ins and watch history come straight across, with nothing to type on the remote.

Aether

IPTV radio · iPhone

Aether is a radio-first iPhone app for listening to audio stations from your own IPTV subscription, imported playlists, and public radio directories. It filters out TV channels and gives listening, discovery, and now-playing information their own focused experience.

Bring your stations

Connect with Xtream credentials or import an M3U/M3U8 playlist. Account credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain.

Radio-only library

Automatically detect audio stations, tune the filter rules, search by station or genre, and choose whether to include every detected station.

Discover with Aether Dial

Explore public radio by country, state, station name or genre, preview a station, and save it without pasting a stream URL.

Now playing & background audio

See track and artist details with available album art. Listen in the background with Lock Screen and Control Center controls, AirPlay and Bluetooth routing.

Everyday listening

Keep favorites and recent plays, use the mini-player or full Now Playing screen, set a sleep timer, and recover from temporary stream drops with automatic reconnect.

NullSign

iOS signing & install

NullSign signs and installs iOS apps directly on your device using your own Apple signing certificate and provisioning profile. Import an IPA, choose a saved signing identity, and complete the signing workflow locally instead of uploading your app and certificate to a signing website.

Sign on your device

Use your own .p12 certificate and matching .mobileprovision profile to sign an IPA locally. NullSign does not need your certificate uploaded to a remote signing service.

Import IPAs easily

Bring an IPA in from the Files app or from a URL, then select it from the Signer tab.

Manage signing identities

Save multiple certificate and profile pairs, give them names, inspect them, select the one you need, or remove an old identity.

Browse app sources

Add AltStore-compatible repositories to browse their apps and releases from inside NullSign.

Optional tweaks

Import .deb and .dylib tweaks for supported signing workflows, including ElleKit support when required.

Lineup · latest release

Release notes

Here’s what’s new in Lineup on iPhone and Apple TV.

iPhone

v0.19.0 · Build 955

Published from the latest successful main-branch build.

  • Setting up an Apple TV now keeps each show where you’re furthest along. If the TV is a few episodes ahead of your iPhone, it stays there, even if you played an earlier episode on your iPhone more recently.
  • The same goes the other way: if your iPhone is further into a show, the TV moves on to where you are.
  • Episodes and movies you’ve finished on either device still count as watched, and where both have played the same one, the further point wins.

Apple TV

v0.19.0 · Build 688

Published from the latest successful main-branch build.

  • After Set Up with iPhone, a show now picks up on whichever device was further along, so the TV no longer jumps back to an earlier episode you last played on your iPhone.
  • If your iPhone is ahead in a show, the TV moves on to where you are.
  • Episodes and movies finished on either device still count as watched, and where both have played the same one, the further point wins.
