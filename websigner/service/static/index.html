Install NullSign on iPhone

You need an IPA, a .p12 file, its matching .mobileprovision for your iPhone, and the .p12 password if set.

Need signing files? Flarestore ↗ · Arcticsign ↗

  1. Download the IPA. Save NullSign from its app card ↗ to Files → Downloads. These steps also work with another iPhone IPA.
  2. Choose the IPA. Switch to Signer. Under IPA, select the file from Downloads.
  3. Add your signing files. Under .p12 file, select your .p12. Under Provisioning profile, select your .mobileprovision. Enter the .p12 password if set.
  4. Sign. Tap Sign IPA. Keep this page open until it says Signed IPA ready.
  5. Install. Tap Prepare install, then Install app. Confirm Install on your iPhone. Open the app from your Home Screen when it finishes.

Prepare install uploads the signed IPA, including its provisioning profile. The installation link expires after 20 minutes. Your .p12 and password aren’t uploaded.

If the app won’t open

Untrusted Developer: Go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management. Select the signing developer and follow the Trust or Allow & Restart prompts.

Developer Mode Required: Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode. Enable it, restart and confirm. Do this only if iPhone asks.

Verification error: Check your internet connection and confirm with your provider that the certificate is valid and the profile covers this iPhone.

Closing the signer or reloading the page clears your files and password.
